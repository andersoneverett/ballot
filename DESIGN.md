# Design references

Baltimore Ballot is an independent publication. It uses no city seal, mayoral logo, or government lockup.

The City Code identifies Baltimore’s flag colors as black and gold: https://codes.baltimorecity.gov/us/md/cities/baltimore/code/1/35-6

The Mayor’s Office of Communications brand guidelines (2021), color page 6, identify Bmore Gold as #FCB826, Mayoral Black as #000000, Rotunda Gray as #4D4D4D, and City Hall White with RGB 255/255/255. The document has a seven-character white hex typo; the implementation uses standard #FFFFFF.

Source: https://www.baltimorecity.gov/files/brand-guidelines-mayor-brandon-m-scott-2021pdf

Verified November 5, 2026. Colors are used as a civic palette; the site does not claim official city affiliation. Black text on gold and white backgrounds preserves readability; gold is an accent rather than small text on white.
